Career Outlook

Job Market & Earning Potential

Average Pay

$75,000-$120,000

Many network engineers earn strong salaries depending on experience and location.

Job Growth

Strong

Businesses need reliable internet, cloud systems, and secure connections.

Common Employers

IT Firms

Schools, hospitals, government offices, and large companies all need network support.

Why the Job Market Is Good

Network engineers are needed because every business depends on internet, cloud systems, and secure communication. As technology grows, more people need help setting up and maintaining networks.

This career can also lead to other IT roles like systems engineer, cybersecurity analyst, or cloud administrator. It is a strong field for someone who likes technology and problem-solving.
