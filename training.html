Education & Training

Training and Education Needed

Education

  • High school diploma or GED
  • Associate or bachelor's degree in IT, networking, computer science, or a related field
  • Classes in computer hardware, operating systems, and troubleshooting

Helpful Certifications

  • CompTIA Network+
  • CompTIA Security+
  • Cisco CCNA
  • Certified Information Systems Security Professional (CISSP) for advanced work

What Students Learn

Students often learn how to set up routers, switches, firewalls, and wireless networks. They also study IP addressing, network protocols, cabling, and how to solve connection issues.

Hands-on practice is very important. People who are interested in this career should be comfortable working with equipment, testing systems, and learning from mistakes.
